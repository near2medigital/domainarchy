    How Domainarchy works

    Brands rent hexes on a shared board, hold them behind shields and take them from each other. Every rule is built so an advertiser always knows what they bought.

    1. Claim

      Pick an open hex. Rent is prepaid in 4-week blocks and renews automatically.

    2. Shield

      Every claim or capture is shielded for 7 days. That is guaranteed exposure, priced like an ad buy.

    3. Take over

      When the shield drops, anyone can take the hex at 1.2x its last price. Untouched prices fall 5% a week. Clusters of 5 or more cost 20% more to attack.

    4. Refund and credits

      If you lose a hex, unused rent goes back to your card and you get credits worth 40% of what you paid.

    Revenue lines

    • Weekly rent, prepaid and recurring
    • Takeover premiums on every capture
    • Faction naming rights, sold per season
    • Sponsored slots in the ticker and war log
    • Perimeter boards and airship banners for small businesses
    • Tactics: jammers, firewalls and war chests
    • Seasons that reopen the outer rings

    Tactics

    JammerSwitches off a cluster's +20% defence bonus for 48 hours. Costs 10% of the cluster's value, and 25% goes back to the target as credits.
    FirewallBlocks jammers for 7 days. Costs 6% of the cluster's value.
    War chestFaction members pool small amounts until they can afford to jam a fortress together.

    Tactics change prices and odds. They never remove an advert while its shield is up.

    More ways to play

    Loot dropsHolders hide vouchers in their hexes. A visitor opens one, and the holder gets the click as a lead.
    Referral turf warsBrands share a link. Visits lift their defence by up to 10%, which raises the price of taking their hexes.
    RebellionsFree scrip from daily visits can be pooled by a community to claim an open Wildlands hex or one whose price has been falling.
    Flash-sale airshipFor £50 a brand hijacks the airship for 24 hours. Every pass is a neon banner in the board's own style.
    Syndicate profilesEvery holder gets a static profile page with a sponsored link. Full indexing starts after four straight weeks in the Metropolis.
    Hall of FameLost hexes stay in the record. Brands keep their place in the history of each war.

    About this prototype

    Domainarchy is a live territory board where brands rent hexes, defend them and take them from each other. Attention goes where the fighting is, and every hex on the board is a paid, time-boxed placement.

    This page is an interactive prototype. Every brand, price, event and total on it is sample data generated in your browser. No payments, bids or uploads leave this page.

    The launch board has 469 hexes in three zones. The full board grows ring by ring to 4,921 hexes across later seasons.
